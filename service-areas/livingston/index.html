Pest control in Livingston Parish.

Geaux Away Pest Control serves homes and businesses in Livingston Parish, including Denham Springs, Walker, Albany, and the town of Livingston, east of Baton Rouge.

Includes Denham Springs, Walker, Albany, Springfield, and Livingston.

Livingston Parish sits east of Baton Rouge along I-12. Denham Springs and Walker are the larger towns. Albany, Springfield, and the town of Livingston are part of the same service area. Many lots are wooded, and that changes the pest picture: more shade, more moisture, and more ways for a rodent to reach a roof.

Mosquitoes rest in that shade and breed in low spots, ditches, and anything left full of rainwater. Ants and cockroaches still take the kitchen and the bath. Rats and mice use attics, garages, and the gap between a deck and the house. Termites are a real concern in crawl spaces and anywhere wood stays damp.

Carpenter ants can show up in damp wood too. They are not termites. An inspection is how we tell the difference. We treat homes and businesses in the parish. We do not offer wildlife removal, including squirrels in an attic.

Pests we treat in Livingston

These are the pests the climate and the buildings in this parish tend to produce. They are not a count of our calls.

  • Mosquitoes in shaded, wooded yards
  • Ants and cockroaches indoors
  • Rats and mice coming from the tree line into attics and garages
  • Termites, and carpenter ants in damp wood, which an inspection separates
